Meet Dr. Peter

Hi, I’m Dr. Peter.

I’m a New Jersey native who has been passionate about chiropractic for more than 10 years. I attended Christian Brothers Academy in Lincroft, earned my bachelor’s degree from Rutgers University in New Brunswick, and received my Doctor of Chiropractic degree from Sherman College of Chiropractic in South Carolina. I spent five years caring for this community before purchasing the practice in April 2026.

It’s an honor to carry forward a practice Matawan families have trusted since 1984.

My goal is simple: help every person who walks through our doors function at their best while feeling comfortable, informed, and genuinely supported.

I love helping people participate more fully in the lives they care about.

Questions are welcome

Feel comfortable before you arrive.

I’ve never been to a chiropractor. What should I expect?

We listen first, complete a thorough evaluation, explain each step, and prepare recommendations for you.

Do adjustments hurt?

Most patients find adjustments comfortable. Technique and force are adapted to your body.

Can babies, children, and pregnant women be adjusted?

Yes. Techniques are adapted by age, and specialized tables support pregnancy comfort.

Do I need to have pain to benefit from chiropractic?

No. Many people choose proactive care before pain becomes a crisis.

Will insurance help cover care?

We verify benefits and explain every option. Self-pay plans may cost less than a typical copay.

How long does my first visit take?

Usually 30 minutes or less, with time to listen, evaluate, and answer questions.
