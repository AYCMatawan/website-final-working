Your First Visit · Approximately 30 Minutes

We listen, evaluate, and understand you.

  • Consultation and detailed health history

Practical questions

Everything you need before arriving.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable clothing; you will not need to disrobe.

Can I bring my children?

Yes. Families are welcome, and the $30 new-patient offer covers the entire immediate family when everyone attends the first visit together.

Will I be adjusted on the first visit?

The first visit centers on listening and evaluation. An adjustment may occur when clinically appropriate, after the process has been explained, and when you feel comfortable moving forward.

Will insurance help cover care?

Bring your insurance card. We verify benefits and explain your options at the follow-up.

What if I do not have insurance or prefer not to use it?

Affordable self-pay and membership options are available. Many practice members find these fees are less than a typical insurance copay.

What forms should I complete?

Complete any forms sent before your appointment to save time.

Where do I park and how do I find the office?

Free parking is available in the Clock Tower plaza at 245 Main Street. The office is upstairs in Suite 2M.
